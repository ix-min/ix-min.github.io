About

About IxMin

IxMin is a research-oriented space exploring the intersection of Human–Computer Interaction, Artificial Intelligence, and Interactive Systems.

We focus on understanding how people interact with intelligent systems and how technology can become more adaptive, expressive, and human-centered.
